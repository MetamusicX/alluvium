# Conversations

Summaries of interrogative dialogues — when you ask questions about the sedimented layers of your vault and the exchange produces findings, conclusions, decisions, or actionable to-dos worth preserving.

## File Convention

- **Naming:** `YYYY-MM-DD-<topic-slug>.md`
- **Trigger:** Only created when you explicitly ask your AI assistant to save/summarize a conversation.
- **Not automatic** — most chats don't need a file. Only those that crystallize something worth returning to.

## Frontmatter Template

```yaml
---
date: YYYY-MM-DD
topics: []
related: []
decisions: []
todos: []
---
```

## Structure (body)

1. **Context** — What question or thread prompted the conversation (1-2 sentences).
2. **Key Findings** — What emerged from the dialogue.
3. **Conclusions** — Interpretive or strategic takeaways.
4. **Decisions** — Anything resolved or committed to.
5. **To-dos** — Next actions, with assignee if relevant.
